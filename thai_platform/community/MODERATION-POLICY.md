# Community moderation policy (implementation draft)

Status: draft for product review; not legal advice. Do not publish as final terms before Thailand legal review and confirmation of ETDA applicability.

## Public access and accounts

- Posts and comments are publicly readable.
- Creating posts/comments/reports, sending messages, and other account features require an active signed-in account.
- This candidate has no separate user-to-operator messaging feature. Comment reports are stored as private `Report` records and visible to staff in the report review queue.

## Comment reports

- Ten reports from ten distinct signed-in accounts on the same comment trigger immediate temporary hiding.
- Repeated reports by one account do not increase the threshold.
- Each report remains private from public readers and is recorded for staff review.
- Staff review may restore a comment or keep it hidden. Hide/restore actions are role-gated and audited.
- A report count is a review signal, not a finding that the reported content is unlawful.
- No in-product appeal is provided after the staff decision. This does not waive rights to contact competent authorities or seek legal remedies.

## Account closure and data retention

- Account closure and erasure are separate operations.
- Set retention by data category and purpose, with documented legal-retention exceptions and a process for erasure requests.
- Do not treat 90 days as the general retention period; it is a deadline described for covered erasure requests under a PDPC notification, subject to the law's exceptions.
- Legal review must confirm the operator's obligations, data categories, backups, and exact retention schedule.

## Legal applicability checks before publication

- Confirm legal operator identity and whether the service facilitates electronic transactions or otherwise meets the Digital Platform Services decree's scope.
- Confirm operator type, revenue and monthly active-user counts in Thailand, and any specified service category.
- If the decree's terms-and-conditions requirements apply, publish the required support channels, complaint-handling/dispute process, and timeframe. Do not draft an internal no-appeal clause that conflicts with applicable statutory or regulatory remedies.

## User-facing notice draft

Posts and comments are written by users. Respect other people's rights and the law. Do not post private information, impersonation, harassment, threats, unlawful content, or spam. Reports are reviewed by staff; reaching the reporting threshold temporarily hides a comment until staff review. A report does not by itself prove a violation. Staff decisions have no in-product appeal. This does not limit lawful recourse outside the service. Public content can be read without an account; messaging and other account features require sign-in. See the privacy notice for processing purposes, retention periods, and data-rights requests.
