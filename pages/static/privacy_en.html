Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 25 September 2026 · Operator: Keith S. Jun (TeemoBKK) · Contact: freaky0@gmail.com

This notice explains how TeemoBKK handles information on this website. If advertising or analytics services are activated, this notice and the consent setup will be reviewed before they are used.

Information currently processed

  • Browser storage: The site stores your selected theme and a temporary cache of news responses in your browser. This supports display preferences and faster loading. You can remove site storage through your browser settings.
  • News searches and filters: Selected periods, categories, sources and search terms are sent to TeemoBKK's news API to return results. Search terms may appear in the requested URL, so please do not enter sensitive personal information in the search box.
  • Access and security logs: The server may process connection details such as IP address, requested path and time, response status, and browser information for service operation, security and troubleshooting. Access logs rotate by file size; the current configuration keeps five 10 MiB files, so the actual period varies with traffic. Application journal logs are currently configured for a maximum retention of seven days.
  • Administrator login: A 30-day session cookie is set only when an administrator signs in. It is HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict and Secure over HTTPS. Ordinary public page requests do not receive this administrator cookie.
  • External services: Chart preview images may load from TradingView's image service. News and market-view links lead to external publishers. When an image is loaded or an external link is opened, that provider may process connection information under its own privacy notice.

Google advertising and analytics

As of 25 September 2026, no Google AdSense or Google Analytics 4 tags were found in the inspected public page source. The operator plans to use both services, but they are not active on the site at this time.

When activated, Google and its advertising or analytics partners may process cookies or device identifiers, page visits and ad interactions for measurement and, where permitted, personalized advertising. Advertising cookies may be used to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this or other websites. TeemoBKK plans to configure Google's Privacy & Messaging CMP and meet the IAB Europe TCF v2.3 requirements before serving personalized ads to visitors in the EEA, UK or Switzerland. That consent-management service is not active yet; Google tags will not be enabled until the required setup is ready.

For more information, see how Google uses data on partner sites and Google Ads Settings.

How information is used and retained

Access details are used to deliver the site, maintain service reliability and investigate security or technical issues. Browser storage remains on your device until it is cleared or removed by the browser. Email inquiries are handled through the email service used to receive them. Logs are rotated under the server settings described above.

Contact and updates

For questions or requests about information handled by this site, contact freaky0@gmail.com. This notice will be updated if the site activates new data or advertising services or changes how information is handled.
